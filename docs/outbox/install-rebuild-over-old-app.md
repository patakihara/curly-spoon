# Install the rebuild on your phone over 0.2.0

kind: physical
default: nothing changes on your phone until you do it; the rest of the work carries on.

Option A: once it installed as an update, say "it installed" and the session records your
sign-off for the Foundations item "same repository".
Option B: say "it didn't install" with what Droid-ify showed, and the session fixes the release.

Your F-Droid repository now serves Auralis 0.3.0, the first rebuild build. In Droid-ify, refresh
the repositories and update Auralis. It should install over 0.2.0 without uninstalling. It signs
in with your household account and shows every screen with placeholder content. Only a test
track plays, until the next milestone, "It plays".
