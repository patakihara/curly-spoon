# What re-importing a Spotify playlist does

kind: product call
default: re-importing replaces the playlist's songs with the Spotify playlist as it is now, in its order, including dropping any songs you added to it by hand. The playlist keeps its name and its place in your library.

Option A: keep the default. Say "re-import replaces".
Option B: keep songs I added by hand, after the Spotify ones. Say "keep my added songs".
Option C: never re-import over an edited playlist; import it again as a new one. Say "import as new".

When you import a public Spotify playlist, Auralis copies its songs into one of your playlists,
each matched on YouTube Music. Spotify playlists change, so you can import one again later.
Work goes ahead on the default, and nothing about it is irreversible: the playlist can be
imported again.
