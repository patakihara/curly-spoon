# Try It plays and My library: use the web app for a week

kind: physical
default: nothing waits on it; the work goes on with "Play or get anything", and both milestones stay open until you have used the app for a week and signed them off.

The minimal web app now finds and plays everything you own. Open the staging address from the link
in chat, on your phone (added to the home screen) and on your desktop, and sign in with your
household account.

What to try:

- Books, Podcasts and Music: sort by title, author, host or artist, or Random (pick Random again to
  reshuffle). Open a book, show or album and play it.
- A show's episodes: the sort bar says Oldest first for a serial show and Newest first for an
  episodic one; flip it, and autoplay still follows the show's own order.
- Search: suggestions as you type, results on submit, and a result plays.
- Browse: Up next, and Still listening for your second most recent book and show.
- Your listening lists, podcast playlists, The Digest and a custom digest, each playing as you expect.
- Play an episode while another show plays, even after using back: what follows is the played
  show's next episode, and every Play button says what it does.
- Resume on the other device: it picks up where you left off.

Not there yet: requests and getting things you don't own, settings screens, and the polished design,
which comes later.

When you've used it for a week and are happy, say "signed off" for It plays and My library, or for
just one. Anything that annoys you, just say it; it goes to the inbox.
