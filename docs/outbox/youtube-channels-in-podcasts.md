# Should YouTube channels you follow sit in your Podcasts library?

kind: product call
default: a channel you follow comes in as an audio-only show in your Podcasts library, added only from Search.

Option A: say "channels in Podcasts" to keep the default.
Option B: say "channels on their own" and channels get a place of their own, apart from your shows.

You said channels are added from global Search, and the plan now says so: no other page offers
adding one. Where a followed channel then lives was never asked. The plan files it with your
podcasts, so its episodes get the same progress, autoplay, digests and Up next as any show, and the
Audiobookshelf app shows it too.
