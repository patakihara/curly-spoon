# Try It plays: listen through the web app for a week

kind: physical
default: nothing waits on it; the work goes on with M1.release, and It plays stays open until you have used it for a week and signed it off.

The minimal web app now plays your books, podcasts and music from your own servers. Open the
staging address from the link in chat, on your phone (add it to the home screen) and on your
desktop, and sign in with your household account.

What to try:

- Books, Podcasts and Music list what your accounts reach; open a book, a show or an album and play it.
- A book plays across its files without a gap; speed, the sleep timer (including end of chapter) and
  the lock-screen controls work.
- Resume on the other device: it picks up where you left off.
- Play next on a second item, and see it in the queue.

Not there yet: search, Browse beyond what you're in the middle of, settings and sign-out, playing a
single song from an album's track list, and the polished design, which comes later.

When you've used it for a week and are happy, say "signed off" for It plays. Anything that annoys
you, just say it; it goes to the inbox.
