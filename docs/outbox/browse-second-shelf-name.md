# Name Browse's second-most-recent shelf

kind: name
default: Still listening

Option A: keep "Still listening". Say "keep Still listening".
Option B: "Don't forget". Say "call it Don't forget".
Option C: your own name. Say "call it <name>".

Browse now has a shelf after Up next holding your second most recent book and your second most
recent show, each as its next chapter or episode, so a paused book stays in view however many
podcasts came after it. It needs a title. The canvas and the apps use "Still listening" until you
pick another.
