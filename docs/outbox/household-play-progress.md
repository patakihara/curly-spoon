# Where your place is kept when you play someone else's book or show

kind: product call
default: Auralis plays it through its own household account and keeps your place itself; nobody's Audiobookshelf or Jellyfin permissions change.

Search covers the whole household, so you can find a book, show or album that sits in a library your own
Audiobookshelf or Jellyfin account can't open. From "Play or get anything" on, those results play instead
of only being listed.

Option A: keep your place in Auralis only. It works on every device through Auralis, but the
Audiobookshelf and Jellyfin apps don't see it. Say "keep it in Auralis".
Option B: give every household member's own account access to every household library, so your place also
shows in the Audiobookshelf and Jellyfin apps. This changes permissions someone may have set on purpose.
Say "open every library to everyone".
