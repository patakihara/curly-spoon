# What should a podcast episode's or book's main button do?

kind: product call
default: Play: it starts now, and what was playing waits in its queue

Option A: Play. Say "spoken button plays" to pick it.
Option B: Play next. Say "spoken button plays next" to pick it.

The plan sets a song's main button to Play next and an album's to end of queue.
It says nothing for episodes and books, so they get Play until you answer.
The other action stays on long-press, as for music.
