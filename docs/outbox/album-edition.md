# Which edition an album brings

kind: product call
default: the edition most of the album's releases share, usually the standard one; if you kept songs from it, the smallest edition that holds every one of them. A deluxe or other edition comes only when you choose it in the picker.

Option A: keep the default. Say "keep the default edition".
Option B: always the biggest edition, bonus tracks and all. Say "bring the biggest edition".
Option C: always ask me which edition. Say "ask me the edition".

When you add an album, Auralis looks for a lossless torrent of it. Many albums come in several
editions: In Rainbows has the 10-track album, a version with an 8-track bonus disc, and promos.
Auralis must pick one to look for. Work goes ahead on the default, and nothing about it is
irreversible: a different edition can be requested later.
